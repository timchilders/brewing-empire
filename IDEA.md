Brewery Empire, a Unity-based, macro-management and supply-change strategy and tycoon game where you build up your empire from humble beginnings to a globe-spanning beer empire.
- Target Platform: PC (Steam)
- Core Loop: Starting in the pre-industrial era, the player builds a global brewing empire by researching groundbreaking science, managing continental distribution networks, adapting to dynamic consumer tastes, and navigating historical world events.
- Educational Intent: Seamlessly integrate authentic brewing science (microbiology, organic chemistry, thermal dynamics) and industrial history into tooltips, event popups, research unlocks, and flavor text so the player implicitly learns how modern beer was created.
